MET_Example starter collection, version 2

A portable example project for EIDORA Desktop with 5,400 artworks, metadata,
384-pixel source images, four thumbnail sizes, and citation-bearing embeddings:
ResNet50 reduced from 2048 to 256 dimensions and DINO ViT-S/16 reduced from
384 to 128 dimensions using uncentered SVD and L2 normalization. Both saved
transforms and the original model provenance are included.

Includes two ResNet50 projections (UMAP and t-SNE), two snapshots, saved filters,
slicers, appearance and camera settings, Discovery settings, and the saved figure.
Two published k-means group sets are retained from the original full-dimensional
vectors. Their methods reports and the collection README explicitly note that
rerunning clustering on the reduced vectors may produce different groups.

Source: The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access. The archive includes the
source attribution and metadata field dictionary. Resized images and derived
annotations form a modified subset; this is not an official Met product or endorsement.

The starter is bundled with new EIDORA builds and installed offline on first start.
Existing user collections are never overwritten or automatically replaced.
See starter-collection.json for the SHA-256 checksum and exact content counts.
